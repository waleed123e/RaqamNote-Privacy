Raqam Note Privacy Policy

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Raqam Note, by Waleed Inc, helps users track personal debts, lending, payments, business receivables and payables, due dates and related financial records. This policy describes information used by the app and its service providers.

Features such as Recently Deleted / Bin and PDF statements are available in app versions that include them. Updating this policy does not add those features to an older installed version.

1. Information you provide

We may collect and store:

  • Your email address and account identifier.
  • App preferences, such as language, personal/business mode and default currency.
  • Names of people, customers, suppliers or other parties you enter.
  • Record amounts, currencies, dates, payment history, notes, categories and due dates.
  • Optional phone numbers you enter or select for payment reminders.
  • In versions with the Bin, copies of deleted records and linked payments, deletion/expiry dates and temporary operation information needed for safe restoration.

These details support app functionality and synchronization across supported devices.

2. Accounts and authentication

Raqam Note uses Google Firebase Authentication for account creation, email/password sign-in, verification, password reset and Google sign-in. Authentication information is processed by Firebase and Google services.

3. Cloud storage and local cache

Profiles, preferences, personal and business records, saved reminder phone numbers, and Bin records are stored in Google Firebase Firestore. Records are associated with your account. Firestore access rules are intended to restrict access to the account owner.

The app may keep local cached records so previously loaded information can be available when offline and changes can be synchronized. The latest server state may not be available until the device reconnects. Local caches may remain until app storage is cleared, the app is removed, or the operating system clears them.

In versions with save recovery, the app also stores local copies of newly submitted financial records or preference changes until their server save is confirmed, or until you discard the recovery copy. These copies can include names, amounts, notes and preferences, and may remain after an app restart so you can review an unconfirmed save. Discarding a recovery copy does not delete a confirmed cloud record. The recovery copy itself is not automatically submitted as a new record.

If you explicitly request account deletion, the app may keep a local unfinished-deletion marker so it can show recovery instructions when reopened. This marker does not automatically restart deletion. Successfully completed in-app deletion clears that account's save-recovery copies and the marker on the device where it completes. Clearing app storage or uninstalling removes these local recovery items.

4. WhatsApp and SMS payment reminders

You can prepare a payment reminder using a phone number you enter or select. The number may be saved with the associated record in Firestore for reuse. The message can include a person's name, remaining amount, currency, due date and Raqam Note branding or link.

Raqam Note opens the selected messaging app with the prepared reminder. It does not automatically send the message; you decide whether to send it. The selected app and recipient then handle the information under their own practices.

The Android contact picker supplies only the contact information you explicitly select. Raqam Note does not request unrestricted access to your entire contact list.

5. PDF statements and sharing

In versions with this feature, you can select a person or party, currency and date range to generate a PDF statement. The statement may contain that party's name, financial records, dates, notes, totals and balances, with small Raqam Note branding and an app link.

PDF generation occurs on the device using records available to the app. Generated PDFs may be kept temporarily in the app cache. Saving a PDF places a copy in the storage location you select. Sharing sends it to the app or recipient you choose, such as WhatsApp; sharing is not automatic.

Deleting an account or record does not remove PDFs already saved outside the app or copies shared with others. Those copies are controlled by their destination or recipient.

6. Notifications

Local Android notifications remind you about due payments. You can control notification permissions in Android settings.

OneSignal supports push notifications and may process device identifiers, IP address, device information, notification subscription information and app-interaction information. Raqam Note associates its account identifier with OneSignal to support account-related notifications. See the OneSignal Privacy Policy.

7. Advertising and consent

Raqam Note displays banner ads through Google AdMob. The Google Mobile Ads SDK may process IP address, approximate location inferred from IP address, app interactions, diagnostics, advertising identifiers and other device/account identifiers for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention and related purposes.

Where required, Google's User Messaging Platform presents advertising privacy and consent choices. You can use the app's available Ad Privacy Choices option to manage those choices. See Google's Privacy Policy.

8. Data sharing

Raqam Note does not sell users' personal financial records. Service providers process information necessary for their functions: Google Firebase for authentication/database services, Google AdMob for ads, and OneSignal for push notifications.

When you choose to send a reminder, save a PDF or share a statement, the selected messaging app, storage provider or recipient receives the information involved. Advertising and notification services process their service data according to their own privacy practices.

9. Data security

Raqam Note uses authentication and Firestore access controls intended to protect each user's own records. Supported cloud services provide encrypted network connections. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

10. Retention, Bin and account deletion

Active account records are retained until you delete them or the account-deletion process completes.

In versions with the Bin, deleting an individual record moves it and applicable linked payments to Settings → Recently Deleted. You can restore them within 30 days or permanently delete them earlier. After the 30-day recovery period expires, restoration is unavailable. Expired Bin records are removed from cloud storage when the Bin is opened online. If it is not opened with an internet connection, server cleanup may be delayed.

Permanent account deletion has no 30-day Bin recovery period. When it successfully completes, it removes the profile/preferences, personal records, business records, associated names/phone numbers/notes/due dates, Bin archives and operation records where present, and the Firebase Authentication account.

Deletion requires an internet connection and identity confirmation. If the app reports a failure, deletion may be incomplete. Local caches and generated PDFs may remain on devices until local storage is cleared or the app/operating system removes them. Scheduled reminders are cancelled on the device where in-app deletion completes.

Account deletion does not recall messages already sent or PDFs saved/shared outside the app. Service providers handle their own service information under their policies; contact us for questions about associated data-deletion requests.

You can request account deletion without access to the app. Instructions and the request email are on the account-deletion page.

11. Children's privacy

Raqam Note is not specifically designed for children. Users should only provide information they are legally permitted to store and manage.

12. Third-party services

The app relies on Google Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Play services and OneSignal. They process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Relevant links: Google, Firebase, and OneSignal.

13. Policy changes

We may update this policy when app features or data practices change. The latest published version is available at Latest privacy policy.

14. Contact

  • ادائیگی کی یاد دہانی کے لیے آپ کے درج یا منتخب کردہ اختیاری فون نمبر۔
  • بن والے ورژنز میں ڈیلیٹ شدہ ریکارڈ اور متعلقہ ادائیگیوں کی کاپیاں، ڈیلیٹ ہونے اور مدت ختم ہونے کی تاریخیں، اور محفوظ بحالی کے لیے ضروری عارضی معلومات۔

یہ معلومات ایپ کی سہولیات فراہم کرنے اور دستیاب آلات پر ریکارڈ ہم آہنگ رکھنے کے لیے استعمال ہوتی ہیں۔

پروفائل، سیٹنگز، ذاتی اور کاروباری حساب، یاد دہانی کے محفوظ فون نمبر اور بن کے ریکارڈ Google Firebase Firestore میں محفوظ ہوتے ہیں۔ ریکارڈ آپ کے اکاؤنٹ سے منسلک ہوتے ہیں۔ فائر اسٹور کے رسائی کے قواعد کا مقصد رسائی کو اکاؤنٹ کے مالک تک محدود رکھنا ہے۔

ایپ آلے پر ریکارڈ کی عارضی کاپیاں رکھ سکتی ہے تاکہ پہلے سے لوڈ شدہ معلومات آف لائن دستیاب ہوں اور تبدیلیاں بعد میں ہم آہنگ ہو سکیں۔ آلہ دوبارہ آن لائن آنے تک سرور کی تازہ حالت دستیاب نہ ہو سکتی ہے۔ مقامی کیش اس وقت تک رہ سکتا ہے جب تک ایپ کی اسٹوریج صاف نہ ہو، ایپ ہٹا نہ دی جائے یا آپریٹنگ سسٹم اسے صاف نہ کرے۔
