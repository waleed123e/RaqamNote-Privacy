Delete Your Raqam Note Account

Last updated: 13 October 2026

Raqam Note, by Waleed Inc, lets you request permanent deletion of your account and associated app records. Completed account deletion cannot be undone.

Delete from inside the app

  1. Open Raqam Note and go to Settings.
  2. Tap Delete Account and review the deletion notice.
  3. Confirm that you want to continue, then confirm your identity when asked using your password or Google account.
  4. Keep an internet connection available until the app confirms completion. If it reports an error, deletion may be incomplete; follow the message or contact support.

In versions with deletion recovery, reopening the app after an interrupted request may show Account deletion is unfinished. Some records may already be deleted. Confirm your identity and choose Continue account deletion to remove the remaining data; reopening the app alone does not automatically resume deletion. If you cannot continue, use the request email below. Avoid adding new records from another device while deletion is in progress.

Request deletion without the app

Email waleediqbale678@gmail.com with the subject Raqam Note account deletion request.

Send the request from the email address associated with your Raqam Note account where possible. Identify the account you want deleted and say that you want the account and its associated app data permanently deleted. We may need to verify account ownership before processing the request. Sending an email submits a request; it does not instantly delete the account.

Email a deletion request

You can also find our support contact on the official Raqam Note Google Play listing.

Account data deleted

When the account-deletion process successfully completes, it removes:

  • Your Raqam Note profile and saved app preferences.
  • Personal financial records: debts, lending and linked payments.
  • Business sales, purchases, payment records and their linked payments.
  • Names, optional phone numbers, notes, categories and due dates stored with those records.
  • Records in Recently Deleted / Bin, including archived linked payments, and temporary Bin-operation records, where those features are available.
  • The associated Firebase Authentication account.

Account deletion and the 30-day Bin are different

In app versions with the Bin feature, deleting an individual record moves it to Settings → Recently Deleted. It can be restored within 30 days or permanently deleted earlier by the user. After its 30-day recovery period expires, it cannot be restored. Expired records are removed from cloud storage when the Bin is opened with an internet connection; if the Bin is not opened online, that cleanup may be delayed.

Permanent account deletion does not place your account or records in the Bin and does not give them a 30-day recovery period.

Once account deletion is completed, the account and deleted app records cannot be recovered through Raqam Note.

Versions with save recovery retain local review copies of unconfirmed saves. Successful in-app account deletion removes the account's recovery copies and unfinished-deletion marker on the device where it completes. Other devices' local data is not remotely erased; clear their app storage or remove the app if you want to remove local copies.

Device data, shared copies and third-party services

Scheduled Raqam Note reminders on the device where in-app deletion completes are cancelled. Cached data and generated PDFs may remain on a device until its local app storage is cleared, the app is removed, or the operating system clears the cache.

Deleting your account does not remove PDFs you saved outside the app, copies shared with other people, or messages already sent through WhatsApp or SMS. Their storage is controlled by the destination app, recipient or storage provider.

Advertising and notification service providers process their own service data under their privacy practices. See the Raqam Note privacy policy for the services used and contact us for questions about associated data-deletion requests.
